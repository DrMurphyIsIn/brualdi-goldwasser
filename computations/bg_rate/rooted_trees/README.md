# bg_rate/rooted_trees -- the telescoping identity and the zero-slack branches on all small rooted trees

**What it checks:** the telescoping identity g(b) = h(y_b) + sum_v sigma_v at lambda = 1, and that the
branches with zero total slack, sigma(b) = 0, are exactly the leaf, the cherry and A_5. Role: a numerical confirmation (floating point); no
proof uses it.

The program `exhaustive_rooted.py` (with `trees.py`) was written in September 2026, and is archived here, in October 2026, with its docstrings rewritten and the last line of
output printed unrounded. Its output below is from a run made here.

## What is checked

All rooted trees (planted branches) with at most NMAX vertices are generated by size, as multisets of smaller
rooted trees: 1,011,311 for NMAX = 17. For each branch b, with exact rational (Z, y) and floating-point g, h and
vertex slacks sigma_v:

* the telescoping identity g(b) = h(y_b) + sum_v sigma_v: the largest deviation is printed;
* the branches with sum of slacks below 1e-13: they must be exactly the leaf `()`, the cherry `((),)` and A_5;
* the non-atom gap: min over non-atoms of (sum of slacks) - 0.01426 * (number of minimal non-atoms) (compare
  the non-atom gap, `../../bg_nonatom_gap/`), and the smallest sum of slacks of a non-atom;
* for each cap C = 1, ..., 22 (the certified rates eta_C of the rate potential), min of g - eta_C |b| - H(y_b) over the
  branches whose largest number of children is at most C (compare the rate potential, `../`); equality holds at the leaf,
  so the printed minima are rounding errors of order 1e-16.

## How to run

    python3 exhaustive_rooted.py 17
