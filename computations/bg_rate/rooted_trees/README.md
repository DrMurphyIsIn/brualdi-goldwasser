# bg_rate/rooted_trees -- the telescoping identity and the zero-slack branches on all small rooted trees

**Paper item:** equation `eq:bg-tele` and Proposition `prop:bg-zero` (second statement: sigma(b) = 0 exactly
for the leaf, the cherry and A_5); Appendix C, item C3. Role: a numerical confirmation (floating point); no
proof uses it.

The program `exhaustive_rooted.py` (with `trees.py`) was written in September 2026, before the paper's
appendix cited it, and is archived here, in October 2026, with its docstrings rewritten and the last line of
output printed unrounded. Its output below is from a run made here.

## What is checked

All rooted trees (planted branches) with at most NMAX vertices are generated by size, as multisets of smaller
rooted trees: 1,011,311 for NMAX = 17. For each branch b, with exact rational (Z, y) and floating-point g, h and
vertex slacks sigma_v:

* the telescoping identity g(b) = h(y_b) + sum_v sigma_v (`eq:bg-tele`): the largest deviation is printed;
* the branches with sum of slacks below 1e-13: they must be exactly the leaf `()`, the cherry `((),)` and A_5
  (`prop:bg-zero`);
* the non-atom gap: min over non-atoms of (sum of slacks) - 0.01426 * (number of minimal non-atoms) (compare
  `prop:bg-gap`), and the smallest sum of slacks of a non-atom;
* for each cap C = 1, ..., 22 (rates eta_C of table `tab:bg-rate`), min of g - eta_C |b| - H(y_b) over the
  branches whose largest number of children is at most C (compare `lem:bg-rate`); equality holds at the leaf,
  so the printed minima are rounding errors of order 1e-16.
