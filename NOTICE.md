# Notice & provenance

- **Origin.** The Lean formalization (`formalization/R3Cert`) and the certificate generators
  (`certificates/`) were developed in [DrMurphyIsIn/Arda](https://github.com/DrMurphyIsIn/Arda)
  (`proof/formalization/R3Cert`, `proof/verification`) and are ported here verbatim, module names
  unchanged, so every file can be traced back to its development history. Generated Lean files keep
  their original `GENERATED by proof/verification/...` headers (the generators here reproduce them
  byte for byte).
- **Telperion.** `telperion/` is a vendored copy of the Telperion engine (version 0.1.6) from the same
  repository.
- **Lean and Mathlib.** Lean 4 v4.32.0 and Mathlib v4.32.0 (Apache-2.0).
- **The problem.** R. A. Brualdi and J. L. Goldwasser, *Permanent of the Laplacian matrix of trees and
  bipartite graphs*, Discrete Mathematics 48 (1984), 1-21.
- **Prior work on the maximizer.** The conjectured answer of Wu, Dong and Lai, and P. Pant,
  *Counterexamples to a conjecture on Laplacian ratios of trees*, arXiv:2605.14176 (2026).
- **Machine assistance.** The formalization, certificates and generators were produced with extensive
  use of an AI coding assistant (Claude, Anthropic) under the direction of the author. The Lean kernel
  is the sole trusted component: every claim in this repository is either a kernel-checked theorem or
  explicitly labelled otherwise.
